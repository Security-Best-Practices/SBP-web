Our Services

Firewall Management

Advanced next-generation firewall solutions and ongoing management to safeguard your network perimeter. We work with leading NGFW platforms including Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet, and Cisco to design, deploy, and maintain robust perimeter defenses tailored to your environment.

Expert Security Consulting

Keith works directly with your team to assess risks, prioritize investments, and build a security program that fits your business — not a generic framework. Get practical, experienced guidance without the overhead of a large consulting firm.

Security Audits

Comprehensive technical assessments that go beyond checkbox compliance. You receive prioritized, actionable findings so your team knows exactly what to fix first — and why it matters to your specific business risk profile.

Compliance Services

We guide organizations through PCI DSS, HIPAA, SOC 2, and NIST CSF requirements with a practical, business-first approach. Our compliance engagements produce real security improvements — not just documentation that collects dust.

Ransomware Response

When ransomware strikes, every minute counts. We provide rapid incident response and recovery, and help you build lasting defenses through immutable backup strategies, endpoint detection and response (EDR), and network segmentation to contain future attacks.

Security Policy Development

We develop and refine the policies your business needs to operate securely — including Acceptable Use, Incident Response, Password Management, and Vendor Risk policies. Clear, enforceable policies are the foundation of every effective security program.

Our team leverages cutting-edge technologies and industry-leading practices to deliver robust security solutions. We understand that each business is unique, and we tailor our services to meet your specific security requirements.
