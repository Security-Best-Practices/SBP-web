## Writing tips that help SEO

- Put the primary keyword in the title, the `description`, and the first paragraph.
- Use clear `##` subheadings phrased the way people search ("What changes in Phase 2").
- Aim for 900–1,500 words with a practical checklist or table. These earn links and featured snippets.
- Link to one or two related articles and end with a call to action pointing at `/#contact`.
- Cite primary sources (NIST, HHS, DoD, PCI SSC, CISA) for regulatory dates and requirements.
- Update older posts when rules change, and add `updated: YYYY-MM-DD` to the front matter.
